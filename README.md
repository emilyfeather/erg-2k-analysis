# erg-rankings-analysis
I extracted the 2,000 fastest 2k times for both female and male rowers from Concept2's 2026 official rankings and have performed some analysis on the effects of sex and age on erg scores! Also, the graph below displays some statistics on the psychological side of the deeply feared 2k test...

I wondered whether this dataset would display bunching just under round number times, where athletes had set goals and calculated precise splits to hold throughout the 2000m. It was pretty obvious at the 7 minute mark for the men...
